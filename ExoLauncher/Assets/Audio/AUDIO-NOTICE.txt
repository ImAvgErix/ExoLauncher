Exo Launcher achievement notification sounds

exo-trophy-bronze.wav, exo-trophy-silver.wav, exo-trophy-gold.wav, and
exo-trophy-platinum.wav are original, procedurally synthesized Exo Launcher
assets generated by tools/make-trophy-cues.py. Format is 48 kHz stereo 16-bit
PCM. They contain no third-party samples and require no external attribution.
The legacy exo-achievement-unlock.wav file is a copy of the gold cue kept for
older installs.
