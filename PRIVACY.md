# Exo Launcher privacy statement

**Exo Launcher is local-first. No account, no ads, no analytics by default.**

## What stays on your machine

- Settings live under `%LocalAppData%\ExoLauncher`.
- Library discovery reads local install paths, manifests, and registry keys.
- Nothing about your library or play sessions is transmitted anywhere by default.

## What touches the network

| Purpose | When |
| --- | --- |
| Official redistributable pages | Only when you press **Official installer** in Dependencies |
| Future update check (if added) | Only with consent |

There is no telemetry, crash-upload, or advertising SDK in Phase 1.

## What Exo Launcher never does

- No account creation or sign-in.
- No silent downloads or elevated installs without consent.
- No collection of friends lists, chat, or payment data.
- No kernel drivers or anti-cheat interference.
