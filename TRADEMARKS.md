"Exo", "Exo Launcher", "Exo Mouse", "Exo Capture", "Exo Shaders" and the Exo logo name the official software made by Erix (UhhErix). The licenses of the add-ons (GPL and AGPL) give you rights to their code; they do not give you these names or the logo.

- Say that something works with, or is a review, guide, video or tutorial about, Exo Launcher, using its name as plain text.
- Build an open-source add-on (Exo Mouse, Exo Shaders, Exo Shield) from its own source and run it.
- Publish a fork or changed version of an add-on under its own license, if it carries a different name and logo and says plainly that it is not the official Exo.
- Link to the official download: <https://github.com/UhhErix/ExoLauncher/releases/latest>.

## You may not

- Distribute Exo Launcher or a changed version of it under the Exo name, or under a name or logo that could be taken for it ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).
- Present your build, site, installer or service as the official Exo, or as endorsed by it.
- Use the Exo name or logo for a different product.

The official installer is the one linked from [this repository](https://github.com/UhhErix/ExoLauncher). Anything else, including a build you were sent, is not it.

Other companies' names and marks that Exo mentions (Steam, Epic, Discord, Spotify, NVIDIA and the rest) belong to their owners; Exo Launcher is not affiliated with them.
