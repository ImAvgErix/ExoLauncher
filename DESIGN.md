# Design

Exo is judged on five things. Every visual decision answers to one of them.

1. **Immersive.** The game's art is the picture; Exo is the frame. Chrome stays out of the way, and art gets the light.
2. **Clean.** Nothing on screen that is not doing a job. No hint copy, no ledes, no eyebrow labels, no decoration.
3. **Modern.** Rounded, quiet, fast. Motion is welcome anywhere it is done right: it shows work happening, change landing or things arriving, answers at once and settles (Exo's two curves), never bounces or adds shadows, and under Reduce motion only fades.
4. **Only backgrounds are the page.** The page, each room's background, is the Appearance's page color (`#000000` on Void); everything on it is Exo's Liquid Glass (Glass): panels, sheets, cards, dialogs, menus, popovers, Ctrl+K, notices, the Bar, Music's player and every control. Over black, glass stays dark, never a flat gray; over art it carries the art's color and bends it at its edge; no word on it ever drops below its contrast. One light hairline sets a surface apart, and cards inside glass are clear with a hairline, neither gray nor black holes in it.
5. **Consistent.** One vocabulary of rows, pills, hairlines and states, used the same way in every room.

## Exo's language

Exo takes what makes Apple's whole family great (its systems and its apps: Music, TV, App Store, Games, Photos, Maps, Weather, Fitness) and builds its own from it, not a copy of any one screen. Five rules, everywhere:

1. **Content is the interface.** Art is the biggest thing on every screen; chrome is small, quiet and the same in every room. The page itself is one flat color (pure black on Void): no glow, no tint.
2. **One bar, for what applies everywhere.** The top band holds **Search** on the left (the Exo mark, the one way to find anything; Ctrl+K), the **Rooms** centered between the live capsules, and **Tools** on the right (Capture, Settings, then the window controls after a hairline, all in one capsule). A room's own controls (sort, Wishlist, the Store's search) stay in the room, in the Bar's rhythm. The bar shares its width and compacts in steps on smaller windows; nothing overlaps.
3. **Live beside the rooms.** What is happening right now has its own capsule beside the rooms (Dynamic Island, Live Activities): the call on the left, the song and the mouse on the right. Each has a slot of its own and slides in and out there, so the tabs never move. The Exo bar is Exo's progress (Motion › Progress).
4. **Float means one thing.** Controls are small glass; panels, sheets, menus, popovers and notices are large glass; panels are 18px, cards 14px, sheets 24px. A pane's glass is a layer under its content, so what sits on it (its controls, a menu or dialog opened in it) is glass too; glass that blurs on its own element (a menu, a dialog) holds lifts of itself, never glass inside it.
5. **Feel.** Every press gives way a little (0.97, 120ms); things that change size morph (220ms); pages fade (160ms); nothing waits on the network to draw (cache first).

## Grammar

- **Rows.** Label left, control on a shared right edge, one hairline above. At least 62px in Settings and setup, 42px where space is tight.
- **Pills.** Segmented choices are one pill with the current option filled `--exo-selected`. Used for music service, permissions, store chips, sort.
- **Controls.** Small Liquid Glass: clear (`--exo-fill`) over the glass's blur and tone (`--exo-control-glass`), edged by Glassy UI's lit rim (`--exo-rim`: a hairline lit from the top left with a softer counter light; fields `--exo-field-rim`, shaded at the top). No outline ring. Fields (search, text, the Discord composer) are the same glass, their words on the true center. Hover lifts the glass (`--hover-fill`); a press takes the pressed lift (`--exo-rim-pressed` keeps the edge, a touch quieter) and gives way (0.97), with no movement under Reduce motion. Every rule that draws a control with `--exo-fill` is listed for its glass in `glass.css` (`npm run glass:controls` writes the list, `lint:css` checks it). Controls repeated down a scrolling list (a song's More, a friend's Message) are a lift of the page instead: on black they look the same, and a backdrop per row cost frames. Inside glass that blurs on its own element (a menu, a dialog, a Bar capsule) a control is a lift of that glass. Primary action is the one control in the ink's color (white on Void). 28px compact, 32px standard, 44px primary. One fill per role in every room (Exo Mouse draws the same roles solid, from its own copies of the tokens):

  | Role | Rest | Hover | Pressed |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Primary | `--color-fg`, text `--color-primary-fg` | `--exo-primary-hover` | the same, giving way (0.97) |
  | Raised (secondary, chips, fields, segment groups, Bar capsules) | `--exo-fill` with `--exo-rim` (fields `--exo-field-rim`) | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed`, `--exo-rim-pressed` |
  | Quiet (icon buttons, tabs, menu rows) | transparent | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed` |
  | Danger | the glass, text `--color-bad` | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed` |
  | Current (tab, segment, row, a toggled icon) | `--exo-selected` | | |

  No outline ring on a control and no flat gray pill: a raised control is the glass, a quiet one bare. No rim or halo on a primary. `exo/control-fill` fails a control whose fill is a literal gray.

  One set of buttons, in every room: `.exo-set-btn` for every button (32px, 13px Medium; `.is-primary`, `.is-danger`, `.is-compact` 28px), `<Button variant="primary">` for a page's lone primary (first run, Setup, a signed-out room), `<Button variant="ghost">` for a quiet one (the Bar's icons, Back, a sheet's close); the game sheet's Play is its 44px `.exo-play`. A choice of a few is one segmented pill (`.exo-shelf-chips` holding `.exo-shelf-chip`s, the current one `aria-pressed`), an on/off is `components/Switch.tsx`; never a native checkbox or radio. ESLint fails anything else.
- **Sliders.** One: `components/ui/Slider.tsx`, drawn once in `ui/src/styles/chrome.css` (Exo Mouse copies it value for value). A 4px track filled white up to the value over `--exo-track`, a 14px white knob; compact (3px, 10px, the knob under the pointer) in the Bar, vertical for the equalizer, bare over a track of its own (Discord's input sensitivity, whose live level is the track). `exo/one-slider` fails a range drawn anywhere else.
- **Art.** The wrapper is the art's one rounded clip: no second clip inside it, no fill under the picture (a placeholder shows only with no picture) and no ring, border or inset shadow on its edge, which shows only in the antialiased corner as a ragged rim. Focus, selected and playing rings are an outline 2px outside the art (hover lifts a cover instead: Motion › Hover). `exo/art-edge` fails a ring on an art selector.
- **States.** A word on the right edge in muted gray; green only when something is genuinely on or running. Errors read in `Bad` directly under the control that caused them.
- **Type.** Sentence case everywhere. Controls 13px Medium (500), body 400, labels 10 to 12px Medium; section names are the only bold (600), titles 650 in the Display cut. No letter-spacing on lowercase: Inter spaces itself and its Display cut already tightens titles, and in Chromium any letter-spacing turns off Inter's contextual forms (the colon in 12:30, the arrow in ->). Small words on small glass stay Medium, since a blurred, moving backdrop is busier than black. No tracked capitals, no uppercase labels, no emoji.
- **Hover.** The hover fill (`#1a1a1a`, `--hover-fill`) on a control, 120ms (a menu row lights at once); a game's cover lifts toward you and takes a little light (Motion › Hover). Never a see-through white wash, never a shadow, and a control never scales on hover; a control shown with its row or card fades in and out on the quick time, and a scrim may lift off art. On glass the hover and current fills are lifts of the glass itself (glass.css redefines `--hover-fill` and `--exo-selected` there), so they stay lighter than the glass over bright art instead of reading as dark holes. Keyboard focus is a 2px white ring; on glass it sits inside the edge.
- **Size.** Three sizes: 20 for titles, 13 for body and controls, 12 for small text, each `calc(Npx * var(--exo-scale, 1))`. The only others are the page titles (game sheet and Music's pages 28, first run 32), the no-art cover title and Discord's emoji picker glyphs, listed in `ui/css-design.config.mjs`.
- **Panels.** Large glass with one light hairline, 18px when they float, 14px as cards; 26/32px padding. A panel with a section list (Settings, Discord's settings, Properties) holds the list as a clear box with a hairline 12px inside its outline, its corners the outer radius less 12px. Fill the window; a page that fits without scrolling gets no sidebar and no disclosure.

## System

One look for every room's controls, from Apple's iOS 26 and macOS Tahoe (`ui/src/styles/system.css`, imported after the rooms' own files):

- **Glass on the page.** Buttons, fields, chips and segment groups are small glass (Grammar › Controls), panels large glass; only the page under them is flat. Hover lifts them (`--hover-fill`, #1a1a1a over black). Primary is the ink's color.
- **One selected gray.** The current tab, row or segment is `--exo-selected` (#262626) everywhere, and the glide carries it.
- **One hairline.** Panels and rows divide with `--exo-float-line` (#1f1f1f).
- **Floating panels.** A room's sidebar and side panel (Discord's members and Active Now, Music's Now playing) float as rounded panes of large glass with a hairline (18px), inset `--exo-gap` (10px) from the window and each other, the same as the Bar's capsules, so their edges line up under it. Content stays on the page. Cards in a page take 16px 20px padding. Settings' grouped lists are clear boxes on their pane. Discord's bar (you, mute, deafen, settings, a call in progress above it) is one glass dock across the foot of the server rail and the sidebar, your picture on the servers' column; the sidebar ends a gap above it (nothing scrolls under it to show past its edges, no two outlines meet) and the servers scroll under it. Through a server change it holds still with the rail.
- **Top bar.** No band: the mark, the rooms (with a lit selection), the song and your tools each float as a 36px capsule of small Liquid Glass. The window controls close the Tools capsule, after a hairline. The Library scrolls beneath it (Glass › Scroll edge).
- **Switches.** 40×24 with a round white knob; gray off, green on.
- **Sheets** round to 24px; featured art (Continue, the Store's hero), popovers and dialogs to 18px; cards and menus to 14px. Art has no hairline (Grammar › Art).
- **Menus.** One way everywhere (Exo's, Discord's, Exo Mouse's): large Liquid Glass (Exo Mouse's, inside its own frame, its pane color with one hairline), 34px rows rounded 8px, the hover lift under the pointer, a check beside the current choice, sections with a quiet heading. A submenu opens beside its row in the top layer, so it is real glass; it flips to the other side at the window's edge and keeps its row lit. Anything taller than the window scrolls.
- **Rooms.** Library, Store, Discord, Music, PC (this PC: its graphics, display, performance and apps) and Devices (what you plug in).
- **One layout on every screen.** The page is laid out as on a 1080p screen (in Windows' scaled pixels) and drawn larger on a bigger one (MainWindow.PageScale: Chromium's device emulation, the window's size over the zoom), so a 1440p or 4K screen shows what a 1080p one does, sharper; a 1080p screen or a smaller one is drawn as it is.
- **Labels sit on their true center.** Controls draw at Inter's normal line-height (a taller one draws the label above the middle in WebView2); a small fixed pill (a count, a badge) centers its text in a grid at `line-height: normal`; a group of segments has no border, so its padding alone centers them.

## Glass

Exo's Liquid Glass, after Apple's, in Glassy UI's material (`docs/architecture/liquid-glass.md` has the study and the Chromium details). Only the page is a flat color: every surface and control on it is glass, and glass never carries a word that isn't on a control or in a panel.

- **Legible by its tone.** Glass blurs what is behind it, tones it down (brightness, contrast and saturation in CSS's own functions, values in `ui/src/tokens.css`, rules in `ui/src/styles/glass.css`) and lays its slab over it, so whatever passes beneath, its words keep their contrast. Over pure black it stays dark, never a flat gray, and its rim draws it. On glass, secondary words brighten (`--exo-glass-secondary`, brighter than Muted, which glass uses in its place) and a hovered row's go full ink; badges over cover art take a deeper dim. The E2E suite measures it on the installed app from the screen's own pixels in every Appearance (`appearance.glass-*` slides a sheet under the Bar; `rooms` and `appearance` measure every word against what is behind it: under 3:1 fails, under 4.5:1 warns).
- **Regular glass, one tone.** As Apple's regular glass, it is neither forced black nor see-through: it carries the color of what is under it, toned down by its band, so its words keep their contrast over any art. It never flips to light glass over bright content (a capsule turning white over one cover and back read as two controls, and changed the Bar's look as you scrolled); on Light every surface is Light's frost from the start. Glass on art (Continue's title plates and More, a sheet's close) is the same glass.
- **Two sizes.** Small glass (every control, the Bar's capsules, Music's player, Continue's More, a sheet's close button): Glassy's slab over a 12px blur, the rim and a soft shadow; the Bar's, Music's player's and buttons over art also take a lens. Large glass (menus, popovers, Ctrl+K, notices, Properties, Discord's menus, pickers and dialogs): Glassy's floating layer, frosted on the element itself (20px), a thicker fill, the deeper shadow; dark on Void and Dark, a white frost on Light. Panes (a room's side panels and page list, the PC room's panels, Discord's dock and profile card, the game sheet and the pages it hosts) are large glass as a layer under their content (14px), so what sits on them stays glass.
- **Its edge.** As Apple's glass of 27: a darkened edge holds the glass's shape over bright art, a light hairline sits just inside it, and a brighter specular line runs along its top (Light keeps its dark hairline, a touch firmer). The lens bends small glass by its size and then holds, so every capsule and button bends alike. The edge is never covered: a sheet that lays art under its words (a game's, a store page's) draws its rim again above that art.
- **The lens.** Small glass bends what is behind it at its edge (`ui/src/lib/glass.ts`): a map from the element's own shape, a squircle bezel with Snell refraction (n 1.5), in two branches, a nearly sharp bent rim over the soft middle where the words are. The map has at least two pixels to each of the screen's, laid over the glass's exact box, and the bent rim is smoothed by half a pixel, so the bend never steps or reads as pixelated, at any display scale. Focus rings sit inside lens glass: an outer one would move the lens.
- **The rim.** Glassy UI's lit edge (`--glassy-ring`, `--glassy-rim`): a hairline lit from the top left with a softer counter light, shade just inside and a faint blue and pink split; on Light a dark hairline holds the edge on the light page. A sheen at the top (`--glassy-sheen`); grain under the words so dark gradients don't band.
- **Light, not tint.** A press lights the glass from within where it lands, and the light spreads and fades as it lets go. Glass adds no color beyond its rim's faint split; a Library folder's flap is the one tinted glass, and the primary stays a solid control.
- **Glass or Solid.** Settings › General › Material. Glass is Liquid Glass; Solid draws no backdrop (controls `--exo-solid-fill` under their rim, large glass `--exo-solid-panel`, never a sheet's transparent float, panes the page). Only that setting and Windows' contrast settings change it: not a running game, not Exo in the background, not Windows' Transparency effects switch (mostly turned off for speed; Solid is the choice for anyone who needs glass gone). Increased contrast draws glass as the page with a full-ink border and every word full ink; forced colors draw Canvas with a CanvasText outline.

**Scroll edge.** The Library runs to the window's top and scrolls beneath the Bar and stays sharp all the way: no dimming and no fade, here or at any page's edge. The glass carries the separation, its band keeping the Bar's words legible over any art; at rest nothing is under the Bar. (Apple dims or blurs a scroll edge, and made it stronger in 2026 for text near the bars; Exo's glass keeps every word legible without it, and the owner chose sharp content.)

**App icon.** The mark as Liquid Glass, built the way Icon Composer builds an icon (`tools/make-logo.py` draws every size, the installer's screens and the web marks from one geometry): a continuous-curvature squircle of black glass, graphite at the top fading to black, with a sheen high in it, a specular line along its top and a fainter catch along its foot; the three bars as slabs of white glass, each lit from above, with softened corners, a shadow under them and a faint bloom on the black. Detail follows size: at 32px and under the plate is a plain gradient and the bars flat white on whole pixels, so the taskbar stays crisp. The mark inside the app stays the bare bars in the ink.

**Icons.** Tabler (tabler.io/icons, MIT, through Iconify's `@iconify-json/tabler`), one family everywhere: Exo, Exo Mouse, setup, and the checks that draw themselves in. `ui/src/brand/icons.tsx` maps Exo's names to Tabler's glyphs and `npm run icons` writes only those into `brand/tabler.ts`. One glyph per meaning: two things that mean different things never share one (the Devices room is a mouse; a server's members and your friends, a like and a wishlist each have their own; a voice channel takes Discord's own speaker). Tabler's line drawn at 1.875 on its 24 grid, 1.25px at 16px: 1px reads gray beside 13px labels on black at 100% scaling and 1.33 heavy. Pick the simplest glyph that says it. Play, pause, stop and skip are solid, as players draw them. Outline by default; a toggle that is on (liked, starred, pinned) and the current room take Tabler's -filled of the same glyph where it draws one. A private channel's glyph carries a small solid lock at its corner, the glyph cleared under it. In Exo's menus a row's glyph leads its label; in Discord's it sits at the row's end, as Discord draws it, so a menu where only some rows have one still lines up. Brand marks (stores, Discord, Spotify) stay their own.

**The Exo Island.** A glass capsule left of the room tabs, only while something is live that the current room isn't showing: a Discord call outside the Discord room (the server's icon, the channel over the server, the faces in the call with a green ring while one speaks, mute, deafen, leave); downloads show in the Library. The song has its own capsule right of the tabs. Each capsule has a fixed slot, slides down into it when it starts and back up when it ends, so the tabs never move. Nothing live, nothing drawn.

**Ctrl+K.** One bar for everything: rooms, games, actions, music controls, Discord DMs and servers. Enter does the obvious thing.

## Motion

Motion answers what you did, at once, and settles; it never asks to be watched. Tokens in `ui/src/tokens.css` (Exo Mouse copies them):

- **Two curves.** What appears, fades or changes state eases out (`--ease-out`, `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`: fast at once, then settling). What travels between two places or changes shape springs (`--ease-spring`, a `linear()` spring from rest, damped at 0.9, so it never visibly overshoots: 0.15%, a twentieth of a pixel on a 24px move; the same shape at every duration). `linear` is for progress only. No bounce anywhere.
- **Five times.** Press 90ms (`--dur-press`), quick 120ms (a press letting go, a hover leaving, anything closing), base 160ms (menus, popovers and glass appearing, a choice, a tab's content), move 220ms (a selection or knob travelling, a page pushing, a sheet settling), flight 300ms (art flying). Nothing the eye follows runs longer, except a room's first arrival.

The moves:

- **Press.** Every capsule control gives way to 0.97 in 90ms and comes back in 120ms, through the `scale` property so it never fights the control's own transform; a pressed fill darkens with it. Glass also lights where it is pressed (90ms in, 420ms out).
- **Hover.** A control's fill comes up in 120ms (the ease-out puts most of it in the first frames). A menu row lights at once, so the row you are on is never half lit, and lets go in 120ms. A control shown with its row or card (a song's Play and More, a capture's star, a banner's close) fades in and out in 120ms, everywhere the same. A game's cover, in the Library, the Store and Shaders, lifts toward you, 3px up, and takes a little light from its own brightness, never a white layer, so its blacks stay black; a scrolling shelf leaves room above for the lift. It never grows: Chromium draws a growing layer soft until it stops, a blur you see on every hover. Nothing else moves on hover; under Reduce motion covers only take the light.
- **Appear.** Large glass (menus, popovers, a profile card, the key popover) grows from its trigger's side, 0.96 to 1 with its opacity on the base time, and leaves by fading on the quick time; a context menu only fades, in 90ms. Sheets and dialogs (Settings, Properties, Discord's settings and dialogs, a playlist's details, the Store's game page) settle in from the middle, 0.98 to 1 on the move time, over a scrim that darkens in by its color; the scrim's dim reaches over the Bar too. Ctrl+K grows from 0.98 on the base time and fades out on the quick one. A Bar capsule drops 8px into its slot from 0.96 (the glass fading in on the base time, the drop on the spring), and goes back up fading on the quick time. A notice comes down 8px from under the Bar into the stack and leaves by fading while its slot closes, so the ones below glide up. Nothing that holds glass fades whole (Glassy UI's backdrop root: glass under a fading parent sees nothing behind it, shows its tint alone and pops to glass as the fade ends): a glass sheet or dialog settles by its size alone, its glass and words there from the first frame; a game page or a room comes up from under a black cover that fades away; rows and buttons arrive by a rise alone; and an entrance fade holds only before it starts (`backwards`), never after it ends. Only glass that floats alone (a menu, a popover) fades itself.
- **Arrive.** Opacity 0 to 1 and an 8px rise, 280ms, 24ms apart, the eighth and later together, so a shelf has landed within half a second. Only the first time a view shows its items; scrolling, sorting or filtering never replays it.
- **Choose.** A selected fill, ring or mark eases in on the base time. A group's one selected fill (the Bar's rooms, Settings, chips, segmented choices) travels to the new choice on the spring, moving and resizing as one shape; a switch's knob travels the same way. A toggle's icon fills in as SF Symbols' do: the fill grows from 0.8 inside the line as it comes up (a liked song, a starred capture, the current room's glass body). Someone speaking gets a green ring that comes and goes on the quick time everywhere (the call's members, tiles and faces, the Island, the Bar's call), so a voice flickering on and off never strobes.
- **Switch.** A tab's content is there at once: a fade would hide the room from its glass. Pictures crossfade on the base time.
- **Move.** One view becoming another uses the browser's View Transitions (`ui/src/lib/viewTransition.ts`, `ui/src/styles/transitions.css`). A room crossfades as it slides 12px toward the tab you chose: the old one gone in 120ms, the new one in on 180ms, its slide on the spring. A page inside a room (the PC room's pages, Store's Discover and Wishlist, Music's pages, Discord's servers and settings) pushes 24px in from the side of what you chose, on the spring (back, quicker). Art that opens something (a game card, a Store tile, a cover, a picture, a capture, the mini player's song) flies to where it lands and back on the spring over the flight time, cropped like the picture and keeping its corners, its two pictures crossfading under it. The Bar, Discord's rail and Music's player stay live through a move. Mid-move every glass surface draws a still tint in its own band instead of blurring (a snapshot shows glass nothing, so it would go clear and pop back), keeping its light or dark look; lib/glass.ts reads again after. Presses go through mid-move. Names are set only for the move and only on what is on screen, so the rooms kept mounted never collide.
- **Waits.** Four kinds, each for its own job and the same everywhere it is used; a wait under 300ms shows nothing.
  - *The bar* (`components/ui/Progress.tsx`, `styles/progress.css`), only for work with a measure: a download, an install or update, a shader compile, an upload, a scan that counts. A Liquid Glass track (the ink at 16% under a light rim) holds the ink's fill, softer at its start and lit at its end, with a sheen running through it every 1.6s; the fill slides in from the left on a transform, linear over 240ms as progress is, and nothing of it is ever drawn past the track's ends. A measured step that doesn't know its amount yet glides a lit third across the track every 1.4s. Done turns it green; a stop short turns it red. Small 3px on a card or art, 4px in a row, a notice or a page, large 6px for a window's one bar. Words beside it say what and how much ("Downloading · 2.1 of 40 GB"), never only a percentage. A transfer's card or row carries it along its foot (`edge`).
  - *The spin*: a control whose glyph names its action turns that glyph while it works (Refresh, Retry, Sync: `exo-spin`). A control of words carries Exo's ring (`components/ui/Spinner.tsx`) before its words, which stay ("Saving changes"). Never a bar in a button.
  - *The shine* (`styles/waits.css`): content on its way stands in its place as clear skeleton cards of its own shape (`exo-skeleton`), and a soft band of light slides across them every 1.6s (`exo-shine`); content being refreshed shines where it is (Library's covers, the Store's banner). No words, no bar, no spinner in the middle of a page.
  - *Nothing*: what is already on screen refreshes quietly behind itself (stale while it revalidates); only what changes moves, as it lands.

  Screen readers get the bar as a progressbar with its label and value, the ring as a status with its label. Under Reduce motion nothing turns, glides or slides: a known bar moves to its place at once, an unknown one holds half lit, a shine holds still and a little lighter.
- **Done.** The art dims, a green check draws itself (360ms) and a 1px ring turns green; after about 2.4s the art and ring ease back. Versions or states that changed stay green.
- **Scroll.** No scrollbar, anywhere in Exo or Exo Mouse: everything scrolls by wheel, trackpad, keys and touch, and nothing is drawn over what you read. A list that goes on shows it by fading into its pane's edge. The browser's scrollbars are hidden everywhere by one rule (`styles/scrollbar.css`); under forced colors Windows' own scrollbars return for whoever needs them.
- **Keys.** What the keyboard does lands at once: a room or page it changes doesn't slide, a menu or Ctrl+K it opens doesn't grow, a selection it moves jumps (`data-exo-input`, `lib/motionPreference.ts`). Art opened with Enter still flies.
- **Glass.** Its filters never animate. A light or dark change is instant for everything on the glass at once, so every frame of it is legible; it changes at most every 250ms and never mid-move.
- **Reduce.** Exo's Reduce motion or Windows' (`data-exo-motion="reduce"`): nothing slides, grows, springs, flies or loops, and presses don't give way. Sheets and their scrims still fade in on the base time, opacity alone, and rooms and pages crossfade, so nothing snaps into being; glass that floats (menus, popovers, Ctrl+K, notices, the Bar's capsules) is there at once, since a fading blur is motion too; a warning's red still flashes, and a jump to a message stays lit. One list in `exo-shell.css` says what still fades.
- **In the background.** Only loops (the bar's sheen and glide, a spin, a shine, someone's typing dots, the Discord Nitro name effects) and the Store's featured-game timer pause while Exo is not focused; they are listed in `tokens.css`. Entries always finish, so a page opened while a game has focus is never stuck invisible.
- **Never.** Bounce, anything spinning but a working control's glyph or ring (Motion › Waits), a control growing on hover, a shadow on hover, a white wash over art, animated blur, animated layout where a transform would do, or motion that loops while nothing is happening. One shake is kept: Discord's unsaved-changes warning, a no as macOS shakes a wrong password.

## Chrome

Floating 36px capsules in a 52px band: Search (the Exo mark; it opens Ctrl+K), the Exo Island (a Discord call outside its room), the room tabs (the current one lit `--exo-selected`), the song's mini player and the mouse, then Tools (Capture and the gear) with the window controls in the same capsule after a hairline. Each live capsule keeps its slot's width and compacts inside it as the window narrows, so nothing ever runs into the tabs. The Bar gives way first (Search to its mark and the mouse to its battery at 1800px, the tab names at 1500px); then the song, by its slot's width, drops its artist, Previous, Next, the volume slider (mute stays) and last its title, keeping play, the cover and, as long as it fits, the title and volume; the call drops its faces, then its clock. The mini player shows while a song is playing, except in the Music room, which has its own player. Every point of the band that is not a control drags the window (the pill's empty space, the gaps, the mini player's time); double-click maximizes; the maximize button opens Windows' snap layouts. The clickable spots are measured again whenever the band changes, including after a glide lands, so a slider never drags the window.

**Confirm.** One question, the same everywhere: Exo's dialog (`components/ui/Dialog.tsx`, `styles/dialog.css`), drawn as Exo's own window asks it (`MainWindow.Confirm.cs`). It asks only before what can't be taken back or costs (a delete, an uninstall, a purchase, leaving with changes unsaved), never to say something happened. Large glass, 18px, 400 to 420px wide, over the scrim: the title (20px, centered) asks it in a few words ("Delete this message?"), one or two lines say what goes and what stays (13px, centered; fields read from the left), then equal capsules on one row, Cancel first and focused, so Enter alone never confirms, and the action named for what it does ("Uninstall", never "OK" or "Yes") last, in red when it deletes. A third choice sits between them. Escape and the scrim cancel. While the action runs its button carries the Exo bar and a refusal shows under the words. A host action the page must not answer for itself (removing an app, a purchase) asks in Exo's own window instead, in the same look. No press-twice buttons, no inline "Are you sure?" rows, no browser `confirm()`.

**Dictation.** A mic inside Exo's text boxes (Discord's message box, its edit and post boxes, the searches, Ctrl+K), at the box's right edge without moving its words. Press it and it turns into Stop while a voice line runs across the box with your voice (a still level bar under Reduce motion); Stop puts the words at the caret (Ctrl+Z takes them back), Escape drops them, a take ends itself after a minute. It runs on this PC only (NVIDIA Parakeet, installed with Exo); nothing you say leaves it. Settings › General › Dictation takes every mic away; Discord's message box keeps that slot for voice messages.

**Notify.** One stack, the same everywhere (`lib/notify.ts`, `shell/NoticeStack.tsx`): what happened that you didn't ask to see, at the top right under the Bar, newest on top, at most four. Each is large glass, 380px: its glyph in its tone (a check for done, an alert in red for a failure) or a person's picture, a bold title when it is about someone or something, where it happened, then one to three lines; its buttons under it at the right; a wait along its foot with the Exo bar. One that passes goes after 4.5s (a Discord message 5s), held while the pointer or focus is on the stack; a failure, one with buttons and one with a wait stay until answered or dismissed (its × top right, shown on hover for the rest). Pressing one that leads somewhere opens it. A newer one about the same thing takes its place and counts up. A result that belongs to an open sheet or row says so there instead (its status line); a copy says "Copied" in the stack, never a flash of its own. Windows' own notifications are for when Exo isn't in front.

## Rooms

Each room is one subject, and everything about that subject lives in it, wherever its code sits: a tool's options live with the tool, and Settings is for Exo itself (appearance, downloads, accounts and keys, about). A room with more than one page has the same frame: its page list on the left (`components/ui/RoomNav.tsx`), a floating pane of glass inset the Bar's gap from the window, one row per page with its glyph on a small tile of the ink, its name and a short line under it, the current page lit; then one page at a time beside it, never a long scroll of every panel. Ctrl+K finds every row in Exo, not only rooms and games, and lands on it lit for a moment.

**Library.** Built around what players do, top to bottom. **Continue**: up to three games you are playing (running, then downloading, then most recently played) as three equal banners of landscape art, each filled edge to edge, with Play one click away and a glass More; its art lights the black around it, a running game glows green, a download or update shows a ring. **Friends are playing**: from Exo's Discord client, one capsule per game you own that friends are in right now, with their faces and Play or Install; it shows only while that is true. **All games**: one grid of everything; not installed is a mark (a lighter scrim and a small download badge), not a separate shelf. Cards are 2:3 art, 14px radius, badge only for Playing, Downloading, Update, Queued. Add game > New folder opens a sheet (its name, one of eight colors, its games ticked from the whole Library with a search); Create folds the sheet into the folder's card at the front of the grid, which glows once in its color. A folder is one card on the grid, first, cover-sized: a folder whose back (with its tab) is its color and whose front flap is small Liquid Glass tinted with that color (Apple's tinted glass; the folder is the one glass Exo tints), bending the covers and the color behind it, holding its first three covers like papers; hovering opens it a little, the flap tipping toward you on the spring as the covers rise and fan out (the one hover that moves more than a lift, as a folder opening; under Reduce motion it stays closed); it opens by growing out of its card into a panel of large glass over the dimmed Library (its name on top, pressed to rename, a pencil to edit; its games as the grid's cards) and folds back into it. **Game sheet.** A sheet (24px) over the dimmed library, art washed across it, as tall as what it holds (it scrolls only in a window too short for it).
