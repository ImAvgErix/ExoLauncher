# Design

Exo is judged on five things. Every visual decision answers to one of them.

1. **Immersive.** The game's art is the picture; Exo is the frame. Chrome stays out of the way, and art gets the light.
2. **Clean.** Nothing on screen that is not doing a job. No hint copy, no ledes, no eyebrow labels, no decoration.
3. **Modern.** Rounded, quiet, fast. Motion is welcome anywhere it is done right: it shows work happening, change landing or things arriving, answers at once and settles (Exo's two curves), never bounces or adds shadows, and under Reduce motion only fades.
4. **Only backgrounds are AMOLED black.** The page, each room's background, is `#000000`; everything on it is Exo's Liquid Glass (Glass): panels, sheets, cards, dialogs, menus, popovers, Ctrl+K, toasts, the Bar, Music's player and every control. Over black, glass stays black, never a flat gray; over art it carries the art's color and bends it at its edge; no word on it ever drops below its contrast. One light hairline sets a surface apart, and cards inside glass are clear with a hairline, neither gray nor black holes in it.
5. **Consistent.** One vocabulary of rows, pills, hairlines and states, used the same way in every room.

## Exo's language

Exo takes what makes Apple's whole family great (its systems and its apps: Music, TV, App Store, Games, Photos, Maps, Weather, Fitness) and builds its own from it, not a copy of any one screen. Five rules, everywhere:

1. **Content is the interface.** Art is the biggest thing on every screen; chrome is small, quiet and the same in every room. The page itself is always pure black: no glow, no tint.
2. **One bar, for what applies everywhere.** The top band holds **Search** on the left (the Exo mark, the one way to find anything; Ctrl+K), the **Rooms** centered between the live capsules, and **Tools** on the right (Capture, Settings, then the window controls after a hairline, all in one capsule). A room's own controls (sort, Wishlist, the Store's search) stay in the room, in the Bar's rhythm. The bar shares its width and compacts in steps on smaller windows; nothing overlaps.
3. **Live beside the rooms.** What is happening right now has its own capsule beside the rooms (Dynamic Island, Live Activities): the call (or, without one, a download) on the left, the song and the mouse on the right. Each has a slot of its own and slides in and out there, so the tabs never move. Fitness's ring is Exo's progress.
4. **Float means one thing.** Controls are small glass; panels, sheets, menus, popovers and toasts are large glass; panels are 18px, cards 14px, sheets 24px. A pane's glass is a layer under its content, so what sits on it (its controls, a menu or dialog opened in it, Discord's dock over the sidebar) is glass too; glass that blurs on its own element (a menu, a dialog) holds lifts of itself, never glass inside it.
5. **Feel.** Every press gives way a little (0.97, 120ms); things that change size morph (220ms); pages fade (160ms); nothing waits on the network to draw (cache first).

## Grammar

- **Rows.** Label left, control on a shared right edge, one hairline above. At least 62px in Settings and setup, 42px where space is tight.
- **Pills.** Segmented choices are one pill with the current option filled `--exo-selected`. Used for music service, permissions, store chips, sort.
- **Controls.** Small Liquid Glass: clear (`--exo-fill`) over the glass's blur and tone (`--exo-control-glass`), with one even hairline all around (`--exo-rim`, fields `--exo-field-rim`); no shading, since on black a lit top or a dark foot reads as an edge missing, not depth. No outline ring. Fields (search, text, the Discord composer) are the same glass, their words on the true center. Hover lifts the glass (`--hover-fill`); a press takes the pressed lift (`--exo-rim-pressed` keeps the edge, a touch quieter) and gives way (0.97), with no movement under Reduce motion. Every rule that draws a control with `--exo-fill` is listed for its glass in `glass.css` (`npm run glass:controls` writes the list, `lint:css` checks it). Controls repeated down a scrolling list (a song's More, a friend's Message) are a lift of the page instead: on black they look the same, and a backdrop per row cost frames. Inside glass that blurs on its own element (a menu, a dialog, a Bar capsule) a control is a lift of that glass. Primary action is the one white control. 28px compact, 32px standard, 44px primary. One fill per role, in every room and in Exo Mouse:

  | Role | Rest | Hover | Pressed |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Primary | `--color-fg`, text `#0a0a0a` | `--exo-primary-hover` | the same, giving way (0.97) |
  | Raised (secondary, chips, fields, segment groups, Bar capsules) | `--exo-fill` with `--exo-rim` (fields `--exo-field-rim`) | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed`, `--exo-rim-pressed` |
  | Quiet (icon buttons, tabs, menu rows) | transparent | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed` |
  | Danger | the glass, text `--color-bad` | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed` |
  | Current (tab, segment, row, a toggled icon) | `--exo-selected` | | |

  No outline ring on a control and no flat gray pill: a raised control is the glass, a quiet one bare. No rim or halo on a primary. `exo/control-fill` fails a control whose fill is a literal gray.
- **Sliders.** One: `components/ui/Slider.tsx`, drawn once in `ui/src/styles/chrome.css` (Exo Mouse copies it value for value). A 4px track filled white up to the value over `--exo-track`, a 14px white knob; compact (3px, 10px, the knob under the pointer) in the Bar, vertical for the equalizer, bare over a track of its own (Discord's input sensitivity, whose live level is the track). `exo/one-slider` fails a range drawn anywhere else.
- **Art.** The wrapper is the art's one rounded clip: no second clip inside it, no fill under the picture (a placeholder shows only with no picture) and no ring, border or inset shadow on its edge, which shows only in the antialiased corner as a ragged rim. Focus, selected and playing rings are an outline 2px outside the art (hover lifts a cover instead: Motion › Hover). `exo/art-edge` fails a ring on an art selector.
- **States.** A word on the right edge in muted gray; green only when something is genuinely on or running. Errors read in `Bad` directly under the control that caused them.
- **Type.** Sentence case everywhere. Controls 13px Medium (500), body 400, labels 10 to 12px Medium; section names are the only bold (600), titles 650 in the Display cut. No letter-spacing on lowercase: Inter spaces itself and its Display cut already tightens titles, and in Chromium any letter-spacing turns off Inter's contextual forms (the colon in 12:30, the arrow in ->). Small words on small glass stay Medium, since a blurred, moving backdrop is busier than black. No tracked capitals, no uppercase labels, no emoji.
- **Hover.** The hover fill (`#1a1a1a`, `--hover-fill`) on a control, 120ms (a menu row lights at once); a game's cover lifts toward you and takes a little light (Motion › Hover). Never a see-through white wash, never a shadow, and a control never scales on hover; a control shown with its row or card fades in and out on the quick time, and a scrim may lift off art. On glass the hover and current fills are lifts of the glass itself (glass.css redefines `--hover-fill` and `--exo-selected` there), so they stay lighter than the glass over bright art instead of reading as dark holes. Keyboard focus is a 2px white ring; on glass it sits inside the edge.
- **Size.** Three sizes: 20 for titles, 13 for body and controls, 12 for small text, each `calc(Npx * var(--exo-scale, 1))`. The only others are the page titles (game sheet 28, first run 32, Settings 26) and the no-art cover title, listed in `ui/css-design.config.mjs`.
- **Panels.** Large glass with one light hairline, 18px when they float, 14px as cards; 26/32px padding. A panel with a section list (Settings, Discord's settings, Properties) holds the list as a clear box with a hairline 12px inside its outline, its corners the outer radius less 12px. Fill the window; a page that fits without scrolling gets no sidebar and no disclosure.

## System

One look for every room's controls, from Apple's iOS 26 and macOS Tahoe on black (`ui/src/styles/system.css`, after every room's own file):

- **Glass on black.** Buttons, fields, chips and segment groups are small glass (Grammar › Controls), panels large glass; only the page under them is black. Hover lifts them (`--hover-fill`, the old #1a1a1a over black). Primary is white.
- **One selected gray.** The current tab, row or segment is `--exo-selected` (#262626) everywhere, and the glide carries it.
- **One hairline.** Panels and rows divide with `--exo-float-line` (#1f1f1f).
- **Floating panels.** A room's sidebar and side panel (Discord's members and Active Now, Music's Now playing) float as rounded panes of large glass with a hairline (18px), inset `--exo-gap` (10px) from the window and each other, the same as the Bar's capsules, so their edges line up under it. Content stays on the page's black. Cards in a page take 16px 20px padding. Settings' grouped lists are clear boxes on their pane. Discord's bar (you, mute, deafen, settings, a call in progress above it) is one glass dock across the foot of the server rail and the sidebar, your picture on the servers' column; the servers and channels scroll under it.
- **Top bar.** No band: the mark, the rooms (with a lit selection), the song and your tools each float as a 36px capsule of small Liquid Glass. The window controls close the Tools capsule, after a hairline. The Library scrolls beneath it (Glass › Scroll edge).
- **Switches.** 40×24 with a round white knob; gray off, green on.
- **Sheets** round to 24px; featured art (Continue, the Store's hero), popovers and dialogs to 18px; cards and menus to 14px. Art has no hairline (Grammar › Art).
- **Menus.** One way everywhere (Exo's, Discord's, Exo Mouse's): large Liquid Glass (Exo Mouse's, inside its own frame, black with one hairline, as glass is over black), 34px rows rounded 8px, the hover lift under the pointer, a check beside the current choice, sections with a quiet heading. A submenu opens beside its row in the top layer, so it is real glass; it flips to the other side at the window's edge and keeps its row lit. Anything taller than the window scrolls.
- **Rooms.** Library, Store, Discord, Music, System (GPU, display, HDR, upscalers, shaders) and Devices (what you plug in).
- **Labels sit on their true center.** Controls draw at Inter's normal line-height (a taller one draws the label above the middle in WebView2); a small fixed pill (a count, a badge) centers its text in a grid at `line-height: normal`; a group of segments has no border, so its padding alone centers them.

## Glass

Exo's Liquid Glass, after Apple's (`docs/architecture/liquid-glass.md` has the study, the numbers and the Chromium details). Only backgrounds are AMOLED black: every surface and control on them is glass, and glass never carries a word that isn't on a control or in a panel.

- **Legible by arithmetic.** Glass blurs what is behind it, saturates it and presses it into a band of brightness (filters in `ui/index.html`, rules in `ui/src/styles/glass.css`). Over pure black, glass is black (it keeps no floor: a 3% one read as gray panels), never a flat gray, and its hairline draws it; over the brightest art its brightest pixel is capped, so at the worst pixel white on dark small glass keeps 6.8:1 and its secondary (`#dbdbdb`, brighter than Muted, which glass uses in its place) 4.9:1, dark words on light glass 9.5:1, and white on large glass 9.6:1 (secondary 5.1:1); a hovered row's secondary words go full ink. Alpha is set whole after the blur, so the edge, where the blur reaches past what the glass can see, never shows raw art. The `glass.legible` E2E check measures it on the installed app over a pure white sheet.
- **Regular glass, adaptive.** As Apple's regular glass, it is neither forced black nor see-through: it carries the color of what is under it inside its band, and the Bar's capsules and Music's player read what is under them (art Exo can read, sampled at 24×24) and turn light with dark words when it is bright and dark again when it isn't, apart enough (past 0.5 of full brightness, back under 0.32) and at most once every 250ms, so they never flicker; the whole Bar takes one tone.
- **Two sizes.** Small glass (every control, the Bar's capsules, Music's player, Continue's More, a sheet's close button): band 0% to 28% (light 70% to 96%), the rim; the Bar's, Music's player's and buttons over art also take a lens. Large glass (panels, sheets, the game sheet and the pages it hosts, Properties, a Discord profile card, menus, popovers, Ctrl+K, toasts, Discord's menus, pickers and dialogs): a thicker pane, always dark (band 0% to 21%), more blur, a white edge and the deeper shadow.
- **The lens.** Small glass bends what is behind it at its edge (`ui/src/lib/glass.ts`): a map from the element's own shape, a squircle bezel with Snell refraction (n 1.5), in two branches, a nearly sharp bent rim over the soft middle where the words are. Chromium measures an SVG backdrop filter against the element's ink overflow, so lens glass has no outer shadow or outer focus ring: an inset darkened edge parts it from bright art (2026's darkened edge) and focus rings sit inside it.
- **The rim.** One even hairline all around (`--exo-glass-rim`; dark on light glass), no lit top or shaded foot; grain under the words so dark gradients don't band.
- **Light, not tint.** A press lights the glass from within where it lands, and the light spreads and fades as it lets go. No color is added to glass; the white primary stays a solid control.
- **Clear, Tinted, Solid.** Settings › General › Glass. Tinted is frosted, more blur in a narrower band (dark 10% to 22%, light 84% to 94%, large 7% to 14%); Solid draws no backdrop. Only that setting and increased contrast change it: not a running game, not Exo in the background, not Windows' Transparency effects switch: it is mostly turned off for speed, and Exo's own Solid is the choice for anyone who needs glass gone (black with a white border, every word full ink). Forced colors draw Canvas with a CanvasText outline.

**Scroll edge.** The Library runs to the window's top and scrolls beneath the Bar and stays sharp all the way: no dimming and no fade, here or at any page's edge. The glass carries the separation, and the Bar turns light over bright art; at rest nothing is under the Bar. (Apple dims or blurs a scroll edge, and made it stronger in 2026 for text near the bars; Exo's band keeps every word legible without it, and the owner chose sharp content.)

**App icon.** The mark as Liquid Glass, built the way Icon Composer builds an icon (`tools/make-logo.py` draws every size, the installer's screens and the web marks from one geometry): a continuous-curvature squircle of black glass, graphite at the top fading to black, with a sheen high in it, a specular line along its top and a fainter catch along its foot; the three bars as slabs of white glass, each lit from above, with softened corners, a shadow under them and a faint bloom on the black. Detail follows size: at 32px and under the plate is a plain gradient and the bars flat white on whole pixels, so the taskbar stays crisp. The mark inside the app stays the bare bars in the ink.

**Icons.** Tabler (tabler.io/icons, MIT, through Iconify's `@iconify-json/tabler`), one family everywhere: Exo, Exo Mouse, setup, and the checks that draw themselves in. `ui/src/brand/icons.tsx` maps Exo's names to Tabler's glyphs and `npm run icons` writes only those into `brand/tabler.ts`. One glyph per meaning: two things that mean different things never share one (the Devices room is a mouse, the top bar's mouse capsule shows its profile (stacked layers); a server's members and your friends, a voice channel and a volume, a like and a wishlist each have their own). Tabler's line drawn at 1.875 on its 24 grid, 1.25px at 16px: 1px reads gray beside 13px labels on black at 100% scaling and 1.33 heavy. Pick the simplest glyph that says it. Play, pause, stop and skip are solid, as players draw them. Outline by default; a toggle that is on (liked, starred, pinned) and the current room take Tabler's -filled of the same glyph where it draws one. A private channel's glyph carries a small solid lock at its corner, the glyph cleared under it. In Exo's menus a row's glyph leads its label; in Discord's it sits at the row's end, as Discord draws it, so a menu where only some rows have one still lines up. Brand marks (stores, Discord, Spotify) stay their own.

**The Exo Island.** A glass capsule left of the room tabs, only while something is live that the current room isn't showing: a Discord call outside the Discord room (the server's icon, the channel over the server, the faces in the call with a green ring while one speaks, mute, deafen, leave), else a download. The song has its own capsule right of the tabs. Each capsule has a fixed slot, slides down into it when it starts and back up when it ends, so the tabs never move. Nothing live, nothing drawn.

**Ctrl+K.** One bar for everything: rooms, games, actions, music controls, Discord DMs and servers. Enter does the obvious thing.

## Motion

Motion answers what you did, at once, and settles; it never asks to be watched. Tokens in `ui/src/tokens.css` (Exo Mouse copies them; the Shaders page copied the earlier values and takes these with its next release):

- **Two curves.** What appears, fades or changes state eases out (`--ease-out`, `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`: fast at once, then settling). What travels between two places or changes shape springs (`--ease-spring`, a `linear()` spring from rest, damped at 0.9, so it never visibly overshoots: 0.15%, a twentieth of a pixel on a 24px move; the same shape at every duration). `linear` is for progress only. No bounce anywhere.
- **Five times.** Press 90ms (`--dur-press`), quick 120ms (a press letting go, a hover leaving, anything closing), base 160ms (menus, popovers and glass appearing, a choice, a tab's content), move 220ms (a selection or knob travelling, a page pushing, a sheet settling), flight 300ms (art flying). Nothing the eye follows runs longer, except a room's first arrival.

The moves:

- **Press.** Every capsule control gives way to 0.97 in 90ms and comes back in 120ms, through the `scale` property so it never fights the control's own transform; a pressed fill darkens with it. Glass also lights where it is pressed (90ms in, 420ms out).
- **Hover.** A control's fill comes up in 120ms (the ease-out puts most of it in the first frames). A menu row lights at once, so the row you are on is never half lit, and lets go in 120ms. A control shown with its row or card (a song's Play and More, a capture's star, a banner's close) fades in and out in 120ms, everywhere the same. A game's cover, in the Library, the Store and Shaders, lifts toward you, 3px up, and takes a little light from its own brightness, never a white layer, so its blacks stay black; a scrolling shelf leaves room above for the lift. It never grows: Chromium draws a growing layer soft until it stops, a blur you see on every hover. Nothing else moves on hover; under Reduce motion covers only take the light.
- **Appear.** Large glass (menus, popovers, a profile card, the key popover) grows from its trigger's side, 0.96 to 1 with its opacity on the base time, and leaves by fading on the quick time; a context menu only fades, in 90ms. Sheets and dialogs (Settings, Properties, Discord's settings and dialogs, a playlist's details, the Store's game page) settle in from the middle, 0.98 to 1 on the move time, over a scrim that darkens in by its color. Ctrl+K grows from 0.98 on the base time and fades out on the quick one. A Bar capsule drops 8px into its slot from 0.96 (the glass fading in on the base time, the drop on the spring), and goes back up fading on the quick time. A toast comes down 8px from under the Bar. The glass itself fades, never a wrapper around it: a fading wrapper hides the page from the glass inside it, so a glass sheet settles by its size alone while its glass and words fade, a game page or a room comes up from under a black cover that fades away, and an entrance fade holds only before it starts (`backwards`), never after it ends.
- **Arrive.** Opacity 0 to 1 and an 8px rise, 280ms, 24ms apart, the eighth and later together, so a shelf has landed within half a second. Only the first time a view shows its items; scrolling, sorting or filtering never replays it.
- **Choose.** A selected fill, ring or mark eases in on the base time. A group's one selected fill (the Bar's rooms, Settings, chips, segmented choices) travels to the new choice on the spring, moving and resizing as one shape; a switch's knob travels the same way. A toggle's icon fills in as SF Symbols' do: the fill grows from 0.8 inside the line as it comes up (a liked song, a starred capture, the current room's glass body). Someone speaking gets a green ring that comes and goes on the quick time everywhere (the call's members, tiles and faces, the Island, the Bar's call), so a voice flickering on and off never strobes.
- **Switch.** A tab's content fades in on the base time.
- **Move.** One view becoming another uses the browser's View Transitions (`ui/src/lib/viewTransition.ts`, `ui/src/styles/transitions.css`). A room crossfades as it slides 12px toward the tab you chose: the old one gone in 120ms, the new one in on 180ms, its slide on the spring. A page inside a room (System, Store's Discover and Wishlist, Music's pages, Discord's servers and settings) pushes 24px in from the side of what you chose, on the spring (back, quicker). Art that opens something (a game card, a Store tile, a cover, a picture, a capture, the mini player's song) flies to where it lands and back on the spring over the flight time, cropped like the picture and keeping its corners, its two pictures crossfading under it. The Bar, Discord's rail and Music's player stay live through a move. Mid-move every glass surface draws a still tint in its own band instead of blurring (a snapshot shows glass nothing, so it would go clear and pop back), keeping its light or dark look; lib/glass.ts reads again after. Presses go through mid-move. Names are set only for the move and only on what is on screen, so the rooms kept mounted never collide.
- **Work.** A soft diagonal band of light crosses the art every 1.4s (linear) while something is being done to it: downloading, updating, compiling. It slides as a layer (a transform), so the art under it never repaints. A progress bar, when there is one, stays under it.
- **Done.** The art dims, a green check draws itself (380ms) and a 1px ring turns green; after about 2.4s the art and ring ease back. Versions or states that changed stay green.
- **Keys.** What the keyboard does lands at once: a room or page it changes doesn't slide, a menu or Ctrl+K it opens doesn't grow, a selection it moves jumps (`data-exo-input`, `lib/motionPreference.ts`). Art opened with Enter still flies.
- **Glass.** Its filters never animate. A light or dark change is instant for everything on the glass at once, so every frame of it is legible; it changes at most every 250ms and never mid-move.
- **Reduce.** Exo's Reduce motion or Windows' (`data-exo-motion="reduce"`): nothing slides, grows, springs, flies or loops, and presses don't give way. What appears still fades in on the base time, opacity alone, and rooms and pages crossfade, so nothing snaps into being; a warning's red still flashes, and a jump to a message stays lit. One list in `exo-shell.css` says what still fades.
- **In the background.** Only loops (spinners, sweeps, the work band, typing dots, the boot mark's breath) and the Store's featured-game timer pause while Exo is not focused; they are listed in `tokens.css`. Entries always finish, so a page opened while a game has focus is never stuck invisible.
- **Never.** Bounce, spin for its own sake, a control growing on hover, a shadow on hover, a white wash over art, animated blur, animated layout where a transform would do, or motion that loops while nothing is happening. One shake is kept: Discord's unsaved-changes warning, a no as macOS shakes a wrong password.

## Chrome

Floating 36px capsules in a 52px band: Search (the Exo mark; it opens Ctrl+K), the Exo Island (a Discord call outside its room, else a download, which opens the Library's queue), the room tabs (the current one lit `--exo-selected`), the song's mini player and the mouse, then Tools (Capture and the gear) with the window controls in the same capsule after a hairline. Each live capsule keeps its slot's width and compacts inside it as the window narrows, so nothing ever runs into the tabs. The Bar gives way first (Search to its mark and the mouse to its battery at 1800px, the tab names at 1500px); then the song, by its slot's width, drops its artist, Previous, Next, the volume slider (mute stays) and last its title, keeping play, the cover and, as long as it fits, the title and volume; the call drops its faces, then its clock. The mini player shows while a song is playing, except in the Music room, which has its own player. Every point of the band that is not a control drags the window (the pill's empty space, the gaps, the mini player's time); double-click maximizes; the maximize button opens Windows' snap layouts. The clickable spots are measured again whenever the band changes, including after a glide lands, so a slider never drags the window.

## Rooms

**Library.** Built around what players do, top to bottom. **Continue**: up to three games you are playing (running, then downloading, then most recently played) as big landscape art, the first widest, with Play one click away and a glass More; its art lights the black around it, a running game glows green, a download or update shows a ring. **Friends are playing**: from Exo's Discord client, one capsule per game you own that friends are in right now, with their faces and Play or Install; it shows only while that is true. **All games**: one grid of everything; not installed is a mark (a lighter scrim and a small download badge), not a separate shelf. Cards are 2:3 art, 14px radius, badge only for Playing, Downloading, Update, Queued. A right-click on a card (and Continue's More) opens its menu: Play or Install, Open folder. Section titles are 20px. Sort, Refresh and Add game sit on the grid's title line. No tabs, no store chips.

**Game sheet.** A 14px sheet over the dimmed library, art washed across it. Cover left, title and store, primary pill, stats in sentence case, tools as one row, upscaler files only when there are any, Graphics (the game's NVIDIA driver profile, one compact row per setting the GPU takes) on an NVIDIA PC. Escape or the scrim closes it.

**Store.** Steam-ranked shelves (big games, new and trending, upcoming, top sellers, deals) under a search field and tab chips. A game page is a sheet like the game sheet over Steam's hero art, shown whole from its top edge and fading into the black only at its foot. One layout for every game, the same places and sizes on every page: on the left the trailer and screenshots (a 16:9 stage at the column's width, smaller only in a short window, over a strip of eight thumbnails) and Details under them as one row of five facts (Reviews, Released, Developer, Publisher, Genres; up to two lines each); on the right a column 30% of the page wide (380 to 520px): the logo's 80px slot and one row of tags, Get on Steam, one row for the other official stores (Only on Steam when there are none), Wishlist and GG.deals, About taking the room left (a long description scrolls inside it) and System requirements, always the same seven rows (OS, Processor, Graphics, Memory, Storage, DirectX, Network) and four columns, a dash where Steam names none. Every block has its place and size from the first frame, with placeholders until the details land, so nothing moves when they do; what arrives fades in where it belongs. A short window scrolls the right column. Cards are 14px, the floating material, 16px 20px padding. System requirements are this PC's: each part in short ("GTX 1660 · RX 590"), a green check on each Minimum and Recommended this PC meets, this PC's own part (green at Recommended, red below Minimum; under the part's name, or a column of its own when the card is wide) and one verdict at the top ("Runs at Recommended on this PC"); a part Exo could not read keeps Steam's words and is not judged. Wishlist is a heart on the page; a price drop on a wishlisted game is one status line, once.

**Settings.** A section list on the left, as Discord's settings, and one column of panels beside it that uses the window's width up to 1080px, so a label and its control never sit a screen apart: General, Downloads, Music, Lucid (when this PC has it, else Get Lucid), Capture, Game stores, Optional keys, About (Discord's settings are Discord's own, in the Discord room). Graphics lives in the System room. Every setting visible; nothing behind a dropdown. Rows are at least 62px with room for one short line saying what the row does. Only rows that change something ship. The section list is pinned to the window's left edge; only the column scrolls, with no visible scrollbar.

**First run.** One centered 620px column, two steps with a progress bar: Your game stores, Music. No key fields and no account in setup.

**Devices.** Exo Mouse fills the room in Exo's look: the page black, its panels glass with a hairline, one pill. The mouse is the hero, a full-height card on the left: its picture large (the work band while a change is written, the done check when it lands, a failed change as a pill at its foot), its name with how it is linked (and when it last answered, once a read is over 30 s old), and a readout of what the mouse reports but nobody sets: battery (voltage or charge state beside it) and signal (dBm beside it) as figures over 4px gauges, a fixed polling rate, firmware for mouse and receiver; one line of four when the card is wide. Each fact shows once: the hero holds what the mouse reports, the panels what you set. The settings take the fewest columns that fit without scrolling, at most 560px each, and the hero takes the width they leave; where one column is a little too tall, neighboring panels pair up side by side in one 920px column (or one beside two stacked), as alike in height as fits. Rows keep 52px, down to 44px; they never stretch, and the last panel of a column reaches the foot. Only when nothing fits do the columns scroll. One panel per thing that mouse supports, nothing else: Sensitivity (DPI number and slider, DPI stages), Performance (polling rate when it is a choice, lift-off distance, motion sync, angle snapping, ripple control, click debounce, power mode, onboard profile), Buttons, Lighting (effect, color, brightness), Power (sleep timer). The Finalmouse Starlight X adds Main switches (Together or Separate sides, mechanical or TMR analog, actuation, rapid trigger, release point, onboard profile) and Receiver (receiver light with its custom color in the same row, Bhop mode, passthrough window); its lift-off presets and custom slider are one row. A setting the mouse fixes is a fact in the hero, not a choice (the Starlight X's polling rate). The polling test and the click test are panels on the page (their pads are what you test on), in a Tests group after Profiles; their reports keep their place, so nothing moves mid-test. HITS speed, where a mouse has it, is a button that opens over the room. Every change is written to the mouse and read back. Several mice: a device pill to switch. No mouse: "No supported mouse found" and one line.

**Music.** Exo's own player for Spotify, YouTube Music and Apple Music, the same look and motion for all three: Home and Songs (every saved song in one windowed list with Filter and a sort menu) above search, your library down the left (Liked Songs first, the service's own filters as chips, its sort orders in one menu, folders that open in place), search above it, the page in the middle (Recently played and home shelves, search results, a playlist, album, podcast or artist with its songs), and a panel on the right when asked for: Now playing with lyrics that follow the song, Queue, and for Spotify Jam, Friend activity and Devices. One player along the bottom, a capsule of small Liquid Glass the page scrolls under, with like, shuffle, transport, repeat and where it is playing from. A song's More menu adds it to the queue, Liked Songs or a playlist, or removes it from your own playlist; on Spotify it also moves it, starts its radio, shows its credits and copies its link, and the library and pages carry Spotify's own management: Create (playlist, folder), Pin, Rename, Move to folder, Delete, Edit details with cover, public, collaborative, Download, an artist's discography, tour dates and About, Liked Songs' genre chips and Spotify's keyboard shortcuts. The title bar's mini player steps aside in this room; the player strip carries the song. Green marks what is on, the song playing, a friend listening now and an action that just landed. A page stays on screen until the next has loaded, then fades in; art keeps its previous picture until the new one has decoded. The service runs hidden behind it as the engine. Signed out, the room is the service's mark and one primary Sign in button on black; the button opens the service's own sign-in (Apple's window, Google's page in an Exo window, Spotify's in the default browser), never its full page; Spotify's own window never shows. A Spotify account without Premium gets one screen that says the room plays with Premium.

**Discord.** Exo's own client, laid out like Discord in Exo's look (DESIGN_NEXT.md): a compact 56px server rail with 40px marks, the sidebar, the conversation and the member list.
