# Supported features

What Exo Launcher does in each area, and what it depends on. Windows 11 x64, on any screen: a 1440p or 4K screen gets the 1080p layout, drawn sharper.

| Area | What Exo does | Depends on, and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steam | Owned, installed and Steam Family games, play time, achievements, install, update, uninstall and launch through Steam; Properties (launch options, language, betas, move, DLC on or off); cloud saves, updates, the overlay and Steam Input kept right per game; switching between the accounts Steam remembers; Steam's notifications and sounds off in one press | The official Steam client, signed in; a Steam Web API key is optional, and without it uninstalled family games read as owned |
| Epic / GOG / Amazon | Library, play time, achievements, install, update, uninstall and launch; Properties (launch options, repair, Epic cloud saves and optional content); Epic games installed by Epic's own launcher or Heroic, and a game Epic's launcher installed updates in that launcher | Legendary, gogdl and Nile, which Exo downloads at fixed, checksummed versions; your sign-in in the store's own page; an Amazon game is removed through the Amazon Games app |
| Riot | Installed games anywhere, update notices, exact sizes, launch; tracker.gg hours with your Riot ID; switching between saved Riot accounts | The official Riot Client; Riot games are never modified |
| Xbox, EA app, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net, Rockstar, itch.io, Minecraft, Roblox, Paradox, Wargaming | Find installed games, launch and uninstall them; Xbox games use their real names, Store art and sizes, in any folder the Xbox app installs to | The store's own client where it needs one; no remote ownership catalog unless the store offers one; an Xbox game is removed in the Xbox app |
| Your own games | Add a game's .exe, or find one in a folder, and launch it | A valid executable; the files stay yours |
| Library | One grid with Continue, Friends are playing (from Discord) and folders in your colors | Friends are playing needs Exo's Discord client signed in |
| Store | Steam-ranked shelves, search, game pages with trailers, regional Steam prices, other official stores' prices, shelves picked from what you play, system requirements checked against this PC, a wishlist with sale notices | Public storefront data; Get opens the store's own app or page to buy |
| PC | Driver and profile: Exo's NVIDIA driver profile for the lowest latency, and the newest driver for the card, looked for at most once an hour (NVIDIA's installed clean and driver-only, AMD's with AMD Software, Intel's without its extras). Display: refresh rate, color format and depth (only what the screen takes), scaling and its override, color sliders, RTX Video. HDR: on only while an HDR game runs, with SDR drawn at gamma 2.2 while HDR is on. This PC: what holds the PC back, Exo's power plan for its processor, and the AMD or Intel chipset driver kept up to date while you're away | Profile, driver and most of Display need an NVIDIA GPU; AMD and Intel get refresh rate and color format where their drivers allow; a Radeon gets Exo's profile through AMD's driver (with AMD Software installed; Fluid Motion Frames per game needs it running), an Intel GPU through Intel's driver (the Arc and Iris Xe driver's control library); the driver comes from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel and is checked for its maker's signature (AMD's and Intel's updates and profiles not yet tried on their GPUs); HDR needs an HDR screen; the chipset driver is updated only where its maker's package is installed (Intel's not yet tried on an Intel PC) |
| Upscalers | Update DLSS, FSR and XeSS files a game already ships, with byte-checked restore; new vendor releases picked up without an Exo update | Files are signature-, version- and checksum-checked; the game must be closed; anti-cheat games only when you allow each one; never Riot games; DLSS presets need an NVIDIA GPU |
| Shaders | Exo Shaders precompiles games' shaders for your current graphics driver when you press Compile, and again after a driver update | Ships with Exo; DirectX 12 and Vulkan games it supports; a game Windows already precompiled for your card is skipped; recordings stay on this PC |
| Captures | Exo Capture: instant replay, recording and snips, trim and export, a storage limit that keeps favorites, and recordings re-saved at about a third of the size after a picture check | Ships with Exo; your graphics card's encoder (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel; making recordings smaller is tried only on NVIDIA, and leaves HDR recordings as they are); OBS Studio's game capture hook only for a game Windows can't capture, never in Riot games or with FACEIT or ESEA |
| Discord | Exo's own Discord client: servers and server folders, DMs, messages, files, forums, polls, emoji with favorites, reactions, invite links, notifications, moderation, and calls with noise suppression, echo cancellation, camera and Go Live | Discord's own account; other clients are against Discord's terms; it signs in by QR code from Discord's phone app |
| Music | Exo's own player for Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube Music; mini player in the top bar; Spotify Jam, Friend activity, devices and Spotify links from Discord | The service's own account and subscription (Spotify Premium); Spotify needs its desktop app, Apple Music and YouTube Music play through their web players |
| Devices | Exo Mouse: DPI, polling, lift-off, switches, lighting, profiles per game for mice OpenMouse supports, wired or on their receiver | A supported mouse or receiver; no vendor software or driver needed |

Exo is verified on a real Windows 11 PC, on screen and with an automated end-to-end run of the installed app. Each changelog entry says how it was checked: Verified: on screen, Verified: end-to-end, or Checked in code only for stores, services and hardware that PC can't test.
