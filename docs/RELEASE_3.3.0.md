# Exo Launcher 3.3.0

New setup and Settings, your whole Steam library without a key, and Discord left exactly as Discord ships it.

- **Setup in three short steps.** Game stores, Music and Discord, and an optional Exo account, with no keys to paste.
- **Settings, rebuilt.** One column with a section list. Settings that did nothing are gone, and keys you paste now actually save.
- **Your whole Steam library, no key needed.** Exo asks your signed-in Steam client what you own, starts Steam quietly when it has a remembered sign-in, and keeps the owned shelf to games only.
- **Epic and GOG sign-in completes**, including Google, Xbox, PlayStation and Steam sign-in.
- **Discord is stock unless you ask.** Turn on Clean up Discord in Settings for Equicord; Exo then repairs a Discord update that never finished. The Friends room is now called Discord.
- **Song saving, trophy pop-ups and launch sounds are gone.** Playback, seek and volume are unchanged.
- **Quieter in the background.** A hidden Discord stops redrawing, hidden apps run in Windows efficiency mode, Exo uses about 40 MB while minimized, and scrolling the library costs the page about half the CPU it did.
- **Exo no longer shares your status or game activity.** Signed in, Exo used to send your online status, the game you were playing and a summary of your library hours. It sends none of that now; the account only signs you in and out, exports and deletes.
- **Messages say what actually happened**, and keyboard and screen-reader use is smoother throughout.
