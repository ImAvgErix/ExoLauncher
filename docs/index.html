Before you jump in

A few useful details.

What do I need to run Exo?

Windows 11 x64. The installer installs per user. Exo uses supported store clients and helpers, so vendor accounts and game ownership still apply. Additional stores support detected local installations; see the feature matrix.

Do I need an Exo account?

No account is required for your local library or launching. Optional Exo email/password accounts are available. Hosted password recovery, email verification, email magic links, and Google sign-in are not currently enabled. Discord and music services use their own accounts and any required subscriptions.

Which devices and upscalers work?

Devices supports specific mice and receivers, with capabilities depending on the hardware. Upscaler tools operate on supported files already included with a game. Swaps are explicit and use validation and backups; game and anti-cheat compatibility is not guaranteed.

Why might Windows show a warning?

The installer is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may display a warning. Download from the official GitHub release.

What does Exo share online?

Online account and provider features contact their services. Local data remains on your PC, while optional account, profile, and presence features have their own privacy controls. Read the privacy policy for details.
