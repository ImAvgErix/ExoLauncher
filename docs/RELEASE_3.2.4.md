# Exo Launcher 3.2.4

Friends and Music now host the real desktop apps, and they use less memory when you are not looking at them.

- **Discord is the real app.** Friends runs your installed Discord desktop client inside Exo's window — voice, video, screen sharing, and updates work as they do in Discord itself.
- **Music is the real app.** Spotify, Apple Music and Cider run their official desktop apps in the Music room. YouTube Music stays on the web player. The mini player talks to Windows media session, so skip and pause work from the top bar.
- **Listen without keeping the whole player hot.** With Music closed and a song still playing, Exo releases the player's GPU and leftover helper pages and keeps the decoder. Skip and opening Music stay instant on every provider. A voice call keeps Discord's compositor.
- **Idle rooms freeze sooner.** Thirty seconds after you leave Friends or Music with nothing playing and no live call, the client is suspended and its memory released. Play, skip, or opening the room wakes it first.
- **Settings, library, and devices.** One-page Settings, library and Discover polish, and more reliable mouse profiles. Updating keeps your settings and sign-ins.
