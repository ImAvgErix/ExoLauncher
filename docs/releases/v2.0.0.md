# Exo Launcher 2.0.0

**Online identity, truthful libraries, and a launcher that stays out of the way.**

Install, update, and play from Exo. Steam, Epic, GOG, and Riot remain the backends. Their windows stay hidden. Progress lives in Exo.

## This release

- **Exo accounts** — email/password, reserved handles, uploaded avatars/banners/GIFs, privacy, friends, blocking, badges, and friends-only presence.
- **Honest ownership** — a linked Steam, Epic, or GOG identity belongs to one Exo profile. Refunds become **Buy again**. Unknown provider data is not treated as offline or owned.
- **Library** — pinned covers, virtualized installed games, compact search. A Now dock only while something is playing, transferring, or awaiting an update.
- **Game page** — Play, artwork replace/refetch/report, folder, verify, remove, and optional signed-vendor upscalers on one surface.
- **Steam / Epic / GOG / Riot** — install, update, uninstall, and launch through the existing official or first-party helpers. Xbox, EA, Ubisoft, Battle.net, Amazon, and Rockstar list and launch proven installs.

Windows 11 x64. Per-user install under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ExoLauncher\app`.

Google sign-in and email magic links are not enabled. Email/password accounts are live.

The installer is not code-signed. Verify the SHA-256 digest on the asset if you want an independent check.
