# Exo Launcher 3.2.7

Exo starts about three times faster, uses less memory, and keeps Discord and your music app working through their own updates.

- **Faster start.** Your library is ready about half a second after the window appears, down from about 1.6 seconds.
- **Less memory.** A playing Spotify behind another room drops about 300 MB of interface memory while the music keeps going, and two leftover ad blockers on Exo's own page are gone.
- **Embedded apps survive their updates.** When Discord, Spotify, Cider or Apple Music restarts to update, Exo picks the new window back up. A prompt the app is waiting on shows in the room instead of hiding.
- **The mini player finds Spotify again**, and it works with Apple Music and YouTube Music too.
- **Exo no longer moves or closes itself.** A process-number mix-up could make Exo treat its own window as part of Cider or Discord.
- **Starting Exo no longer steals focus** from the game or app you were using.
- **Discover and Devices are gone.** Exo is Library, Friends and Music.
- **Smaller and smoother interface.** Hundreds of KB less code, scrolling on the compositor, and no more polling for game state.
- **Riot says what went wrong** when a game needs an update, instead of showing a raw error.
